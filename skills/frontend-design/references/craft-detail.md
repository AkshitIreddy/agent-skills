# Craft detail

Use techniques that serve the chosen direction. These are options to compare,
not a required layer count or a mandate for every interface.

- Materials can combine variation at large, medium, and fine scales with
  coherent lighting, texture, contact shadows, and edge treatment.
- Repeated organic elements can use seeded differences in shape, placement,
  tone, and orientation. Preserve uniformity where it communicates structure.
- Typography, spacing, optical alignment, and restrained color can carry a
  minimal design. Bespoke content matters more than ornamental complexity.
- Motion can use anticipation, follow-through, and settling when these clarify
  an interaction. Preserve interruption, responsiveness, and reduced motion.
- Sound can combine transient, body, texture, and tail when a cue benefits.
  Shape envelopes and measure the result before adding processing. Use the
  available audio-verification workflow for synthesized Web Audio changes.

Compare with the existing result at actual scale and in the integrated product.
Keep the baseline when a variant adds complexity without improving the brief.
For motion or sound, verify temporal behavior and separate offline measurement
from live playback and human perception. A state or ownership defect may justify
a subsystem repair; ornamental polish alone does not imply a rewrite.
